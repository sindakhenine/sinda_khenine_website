# Reader FAQ

## What is the book about?
It is about understanding subconscious patterns as inherited architecture and changing them gradually through the five-layer Living Blueprint.

## Who is it for?
Readers who function capably but notice that some reactions, rules or habits belong to an older situation.

## What is the Living Blueprint?
Ground, Frame, Wiring, Rooms and Facade: five layers for reading inherited context, beliefs, emotional circuits, lived patterns and the shown self.

## Is this therapy or a medical book?
No. It is a companion for reflection and personal growth, not medical or psychological advice or a substitute for professional support.

## What formats will be available?
Ebook, paperback and hardcover are planned. An audiobook is planned later. Exact availability is coming soon.

## Will the audiobook use the author’s voice?
The plan is for Sinda Khenine to narrate in her own voice. It has not been recorded. No synthetic voice is planned.
