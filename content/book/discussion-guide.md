# Reader Discussion Guide

## Introduction and safety

Use this guide for thoughtful, voluntary conversation. Participants may pass on any prompt. This is not a substitute for professional mental-health support.

## Questions

1. What changes when a pattern is viewed as a once-useful structure?
2. Where does the book distinguish reading from fixing?
3. Which metaphor clarified something, and which did not?
4. **Ground:** Which inherited rule is easiest to see only in hindsight?
5. **Ground:** How can a person honor where they came from without preserving every rule?
6. **Frame:** What makes an identity contract load-bearing?
7. **Frame:** What support must exist before an old beam changes?
8. **Wiring:** What is gained by treating a trigger as information?
9. **Wiring:** Why rehearse a response in calm?
10. **Rooms:** Which daily pattern reveals the structure most clearly?
11. **Rooms:** What does one-room-at-a-time protect?
12. **Facade:** When is a facade a boundary rather than a deception?
13. **Facade:** What creates the cost of maintaining two houses?
14. Where does the book refuse overnight transformation?
15. How does engineering language help or limit the discussion?
16. What does “renovation in residence” require in ordinary life?
17. Which exercise would you try, revise or decline?
18. What would holding the pencil mean without controlling every outcome?

## Individual reflection

Write one inherited sentence, what it once protected, its current cost and one small piece of evidence that a newer rule might be safe. Keep private unless you choose to share.

## 60-minute session

5 welcome/safety; 10 opening impressions; 25 select one question per layer; 10 individual reflection; 8 optional sharing; 2 closing.

## Multi-session format

Session 1: Ground/Frame. Session 2: Wiring. Session 3: Rooms/Facade. Session 4: renovation practice and closing.

## Facilitation

Invite, do not interrogate. Avoid diagnosis and advice-giving. Use “I” language. Protect confidentiality, time and the right to pass. Signpost professional support when needed.

## Closing reflection

What can you notice this week before asking yourself to change it?
