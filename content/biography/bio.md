## Full website biography

Sinda Khenine is an AI and data engineer, founder, author and conference speaker. She works where complex systems meet human understanding: in data architecture and AI, in tools that make information usable, and in writing that gives readers a way to see structures that usually remain hidden.

She founded SKatalyst AI to pursue that work in a focused form. Alongside her engineering practice, she developed *The Subconscious Architect*, an independent publishing project built around the Living Blueprint,a five-layer language for examining inherited patterns without treating the self as an opponent.

The subjects differ, but the working principle is shared: good architecture makes complexity legible. Sinda’s professional and creative work asks how systems can return agency to the people who depend on them.
