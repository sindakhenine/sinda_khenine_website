# Legal Placeholders

Professional legal review is required before deployment.

- Data-controller name and service address.
- German Impressum responsible entity, postal address and applicable registration/VAT details.
- Contact/newsletter lawful basis, retention, processor list, data-subject rights and double opt-in evidence.
- Whether Vercel or another host creates international-transfer or processor disclosures.
- Media/copyright credits for portrait, cover and videos.
- Cookie and analytics position after final production integrations.
- Editorial responsibility and dispute-resolution wording.
- Form abuse-prevention provider and associated privacy disclosure.

No registration, address, VAT number or legal claim has been invented. Current `/privacy` and `/imprint` routes are clearly marked placeholders.
